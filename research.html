RESEARCH

Research Experience

Olin Health Equity & Access Lab (HEAL) Jan 2025 – Present

Designing a pulse oximetry circuitry that accounts for melanin content by isolating DC signal components to quantify skin pigmentation and improve SpO2 accuracy. This research addresses racial bias in medical devices.

Key Contributions:

  • Designed circuitry to isolate DC components for melanin detection.
  • Testing and debugging the circuit using custom-made optical phantoms, analyzing and plotting results to validate performance.
  • Working on transitioning the breadboard prototype to PCB to remove noise.
  • Presented research poster at BMES 2025 Annual Meeting.

Publications

Kuhu Jayaswal, Akshat Jain, Rohan Bendapudi, and Chhavi Goenka
"Development of a New Device to Address Racial Bias in Pulse Oximetry"
